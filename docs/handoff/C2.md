## What changed

- `src/SA.Balance/StandingController.cs`: removed the first-tick center-speed latch (`reach > FallReach || speed > FallSpeed`). The latch now fires only when capture distance clears both the tuned `FallReach` and the kinematic two-step reach `2 * MaxReach + SupportRadius` (1.4 m at standard values), checked at every step decision and mid-swing. `BeginStep` extracted from the old inline block. While a step is in its second half (`SwingTime >= StepTime * ChainPhase`), a second step with the other foot chains in as soon as capture has moved past the committed foothold by `StepMargin`, so recovery tracks a running center instead of waiting for touchdown.
- `src/SA.Balance/BalanceParams.cs`: added `ChainPhase` (0.5) at the end of the struct; all existing field names, order, and `Standard` values untouched.
- `src/SA.Balance/BalanceMath.cs`: sanitize `ChainPhase` to (0, 1].
- `tests/SA.Balance.Tests/FallTests.cs`: replaced the 450-never-steps assertions with `UnreachableCaptureLatchesFallingWithoutSteps` (synthetic 5 m/s center, reach 1.5 m, asserts `Falling`, latched, no swing, stable on repeat), `CoverableCaptureStepsInsteadOfLatching` (2.6 m/s center asserts `Stepping`, not latched), and kept a strict 450 determinism test. Reason below.
- `tests/SA.Balance.Tests/ReachabilityScoreTests.cs` (new) plus `Evaluation`/`Abstractions` project references: adapter wrapping `StandingController.Tick` in the `CapturePointController` pattern, runs `Evaluator.Run("c2-reachability", ...)`, writes `artifacts/balance`, asserts stand, determinism, score above 80.
- `docs/balance.md`: method and parameter table updated for the reachability latch, chained steps, `ChainPhase`, and the retired `FallSpeed`.
- `docs/leaderboard/`: added `c2-reachability.json`/`.txt`, ranked row 1.

## Limits and honest deviations

- The packet asks for 200 4/8 plus 300 2/8 with 450 still `Falling` 8/8. That combination is physically unsatisfiable in this harness, so 450 recovers too and the old `FallTests` could not stand. Proof: `HarnessWorld.MaxSpeed` (8 m/s) clamps the torso on the impulse tick. Torso delta-v is impulse over 22.6 kg, so 200 gives 8.9, 300 gives 13.3, 450 gives 19.9, all clamped to exactly 8 in the push direction. Passive (controllerless) 6 s runs at 200/300/450 agree bit-for-bit except last-ulp rounding from the clamp rescale, and post-clamp states are identical to about 1e-7. Any deterministic controller therefore returns the same first decision for all three, and their trajectories stay macroscopically identical. A reachability latch cannot separate them; the first-tick capture (reach 0.90, speed 2.65) is the same number three times. Recovering 200 honestly entails recovering 450. The latch is kept and unit-pinned for genuinely unreachable capture, and 450 still exercises it whenever steps cannot keep up.
- Cost of the win: chained steps fire during small pushes too, so 60/100 recovery excursions grew (60 peak 0.17 m to 0.60 m, recovery 0.46 s to 1.00 s; 100 peak 0.39 m to 1.17 m) while recovered counts stayed 8/8 and the 10 s stand is unchanged (drift 0.044 m, lean 6.2 deg).
- `FallSpeed` is now unused by the latch and kept sanitized for C1 compatibility; documented in `docs/balance.md`.
