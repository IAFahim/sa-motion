## Design decisions for R9 and the integrator

`JointDrive.Target` is a world orientation (relative-spin damping). Torques
come from `QuaternionDrive.Torque`, split proportional-explicit and
derivative-implicit with a single shared `MaxTorque` clamp, applied equal and
opposite on child and parent; the pelvis entry is a world orientation torque.
The tested gain schedule and its rules (topple-gradient stiffness, pelvis
anchor 2000, coupled-inertia critical damping) are in `docs/harness.md`.
Elbow/knee twist maps to the anatomical flexion axis with per-side signs to
match the mirrored profile ranges. Hinge detail: limits are enforced by
inelastic velocity solve plus gentle position projection; contacts and anchors
likewise project once per substep outside the iteration loop, because
projecting inside the loop pumped energy and churned the pile.
