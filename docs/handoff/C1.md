## Limits

- Only `FallSpeed` differs from standard; 200 N-s still drops 1 of 8
  and 300 N-s drops 5 of 8. Further gains need step-reach or timing
  changes, which regressed the hold-out in every trial above.
- 450 N-s now recovers 4 of 8 instead of 0 of 8 (no score effect;
  the latch still fires past the higher speed bound). The B1
  all-fall-at-450 property no longer holds exactly.
- Optimizer wall time dominates: a full 16x8 run is ~13 min; the
  round overran the 45 min packet budget.
