# R5 handoff: bracing against walls and obstacles

## What was built

`src/SA.ReactionControl/Brace/` (new, 5 files) plus `tests/SA.ReactionControl.Tests/Brace/BraceTests.cs` (new, 6 tests). Port record in `docs/port/R5.md`, committed first and kept in sync.

- `BraceParams` with `Standard`: 20 named tuning fields, each mapped from its `kv!` slot in the port record; authored SI starting values.
- `BalanceHint` (`Support` 0..1, `Lean`): replaces the source `Dbm` coupling. No `Dbm` reads or writes remain; R1 wires real values later.
- `BraceState`: caller-owned POD with phase (`Idle`/`Reaching`/`Braced`), per-arm mask, smoothed predicted distance, latched contacts + normals, hold/stagger timers, smoothed lean.
- `Brace.Tick` over `ReadOnlySpan<RoleBody>` plus caller-supplied probes (one wall `SupportProbe`, one contact `SupportProbe` per hand), returning arm `JointDrive`s and a `BraceResult` with per-hand reach points, lean/crouch intent, and contact flag. Total: short spans, non-finite positions, bad `dt` give passive drives and `Idle`, never throw.
- Standing path only: predicted-distance smoothing with engage/release hysteresis, per-arm range hysteresis plus front-hemisphere gate, deterministic stagger of the farther hand on dual engage, shoulder-centered `MaxReach` clamp (no overextension), analytic two-bone reach (shoulder swing, law-of-cosines elbow) and contact-normal wrist alignment, contact latch with hold-then-idle release. Fallen/grab/pedal/roll/head/twist kids excluded per the port record; legs untouched.
- Up is world `+Y`, facing is torso-rotation `+Z`; ordinary float math throughout.
