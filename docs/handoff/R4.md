# R4 handoff: catch fall, contact probes and arm response

## What was built

`src/SA.ReactionControl/CatchFall/` only, plus `tests/SA.ReactionControl.Tests/CatchFall/` and `docs/port/R4.md`:

- `CatchFallParams.cs`: 24 named fields with authored standards, one per game-tuning slot (`LegsStiffness`, `TorsoStiffness`, `ArmsStiffness`, `ForwardMaxArmOffset`, `BackwardsMinArmOffset`, `SpinReductionGain`, `UseHeadLook`, `Reach` 0.70 m, `ProbeLength` 2.0 m, `MinExtension`, `StiffnessFloor`, `GainFloor`, `RelaxRate`, `RecoverRate`, `GainRate`, `ArmCapScale`, `FallBias`, `SpinTorqueScale`, `SpinTorqueMax` 60 N m, `ClavFollowRatio`, `ElbowRest`, `TorqueLimit`, `SafeMissStiffness`, `SpineTwistLimit`). `Standard` provided, no all-zero default.
- `CatchFallState.cs`: caller-owned POD state (`Active`, `Touched`, `Dir`, `Forward`, `Gain`, `Upright`, leg memory, per-arm state), `CatchFallProbes` (one `SupportProbe` plus touching flag per arm), `CatchFallResult` (`HandTargetLeft/Right`, `HeadTarget`, `SpineTwist`, `SpinTorque`, `Touched`).
- `CatchFall.cs`: `Start` (deterministic neutral state, replaces `random`), `Deactivate`, `Tick` (fall direction with downward bias, kinetic-energy gain, reach-clamped per-arm aim, contact-averaged target, per-arm relax/recover scales, aim-rotation drives for all six arm roles, head target plus capped spine twist), `SpinTorque` (opposes up-axis component, capped). Everything else `Passive`. Total, allocation-free, ordinary float math.
- `CatchFallTests.cs` (6 tests): in-reach extension with hand targets exactly on the probe contacts, safe-miss with targets inside `Reach` and low hand stiffness, 60-tick post-contact monotonic relax, spin opposition plus cap, hostile NaN/infinity/huge inputs, start determinism plus deactivation.

Port record `docs/port/R4.md` was committed first and kept in sync (includes the late `kv190` exclusion row).
