delegate, `PushSpec` (Role/Direction/Impulse/AtSeconds) and

with `Run` (one push at 60 Hz: settle 0.5 s, center-hit impulse, sample
every step) and `Sweep` (torso pushes over `impulses * directions` in
impulse-major order, direction k at 2 pi k / n in XZ), plus shared
`Controllers.Passive` and `Controllers.HoldPose` (lazy start-pose
snapshot, H1 gain schedule). Fell is pelvis below 0.5 m or end lean
past 60 deg; RecoverySeconds is time after the push until height stays
within 0.1 m and lean within 15 deg through the end (full window when
never); Steps counts rising foot-lift edges past 0.05 m. Hostile input
(null/throwing controller, bad role, non-finite numbers, bad counts)
runs passive, forces Fell, and keeps every field finite; NaN drives are
sanitized per step. Six xunit facts cover every packet check.

## Design notes for the integrator and R9

Recovery claims are scoped to 5 s windows: the stiff hold has a slow
rocking mode that passes 60 deg lean after ~8 s even unpushed, so longer
windows are non-monotonic in impulse (J = 0 falls at 10 s while J = 100
stands). `Sweep` clamps directions to 360 and empty-returns past 65536
entries; `Run` clamps time to 60 s. `HoldPose()` snapshots the first
world it sees, so one instance works across fresh `Run` worlds.
