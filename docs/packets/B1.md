# B1: Capture-point controller (staged)

Read `docs/packets/B-common.md` first, then this file. Project name: `SA.Balance.CapturePoint`.

Derive from the linear inverted pendulum and capture point (Pratt et al. 2006; Koolen et al. 2012) and the ankle and hip strategies (Kuo and Zajac 1993).

Stages, commit after each:

1. **Hold.** Strong pose hold on every joint, gain-scheduled (see `docs/harness.md`), pelvis height servo. Aim: pass the 10 s stand.
2. **Ankle and hip strategy.** Capture point `xi = com + v / sqrt(g / h)`. If it is inside the support polygon (the two foot contacts) shift the center of pressure by leaning the pelvis and torso against the push.
3. **Stepping.** When `xi` leaves the support polygon plus a margin, place the swing foot at `xi` plus a margin, lift, land, replant, using the leg drives only.
4. **Failure.** When `xi` is unreachable, latch `Falling`, drop to a low tone, stop stepping.
