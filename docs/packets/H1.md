# H1: Pure .NET articulated test harness

Read `AGENTS.md` first. Then this file. They are your whole task.

Owns: the new test-support project `tests/SA.Harness/*` and its tests `tests/SA.Harness.Tests/*`, plus `docs/harness.md`. Create both csproj files (copy the style of `tests/SA.ReactionControl.Tests`, restore once without a lock file and commit the generated `packages.lock.json`). Do not edit `src/`.

This is test support. It is not shipped and not the production solver; Unity Physics remains the only solver in the product. Its purpose is to let agents tune controllers in seconds without Unity.

## Deliver

A deterministic 15-body humanoid simulator: `HarnessWorld` built from `SA.Humanoid` roles and `HumanProfile` masses, capsule-like bodies with fixed sizes you document, ball joints with swing and twist limits, a ground plane with friction, gravity, semi-implicit Euler with sequential-impulse constraints (documented iteration count). API:

- `HarnessWorld.Create(...)`, `Step(float dt)`.
- `ReadOnlySpan<RoleBody> Bodies` in world space (using the shared `SA.ReactionControl.RoleBody`, so add a project reference).
- `SupportProbe ProbeGround(Role foot)`.
- `ApplyDrives(ReadOnlySpan<JointDrive>)` using `SA.Motion.Mechanics.QuaternionDrive.Torque` with equal and opposite impulses on parent and child.
- `ApplyImpulse(Role, Float3 impulse, Float3 worldPoint)` for pushes.

## Check

- Passive: dropped from 1 m onto the ground, it comes to rest, no body penetrates the ground by more than 2 cm, and total energy never increases between steps beyond 1% (numerical tolerance).
- Joints stay connected: anchor separation below 2 cm over 5 s of passive collapse.
- Joint limits hold: no joint exceeds its limit by more than 5 degrees at rest.
- Drives: with strong gains, holding the initial standing pose for 5 s keeps the pelvis within 10 cm of its start (a held pose with a strong drive stands).
- Determinism: two runs with identical inputs produce bit-identical bodies.
- Timestep: 60 Hz and 120 Hz runs of the collapse test agree on resting pose within 5 cm.
- Document the stability limits you found in `docs/harness.md`.

Commands: `eng/dotnet_test.sh tests/SA.Harness.Tests/SA.Harness.Tests.csproj`
