# B7: Deadbeat stepping on top of the hold

Read `docs/packets/B-common.md` first, then this file. Project name: `SA.Balance.Deadbeat`.

Keep the hold controller for standing. When the capture point leaves the support region, compute the single step location that brings the capture point inside after the step duration (Englsberger et al. 2011), generate the swing foot trajectory through leg drives and replant. The point of this packet is the stepping logic; treat the hold as a given and report what the stepping adds at 150, 200 and 300 N·s.
