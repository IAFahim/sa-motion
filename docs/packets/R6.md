## Check

- Zero strength gives `JointDrive.Passive`. Strength scales stiffness linearly and respects the cap.
- `q` and `-q` targets give the same drive. Targets beyond the soft limit are pulled toward the limit with a bounded gain.
- Round trip: angles to quaternion to angles returns the input within 1e-4 away from the singularity, and a defined value at it.
- Hostile inputs give a passive drive.
