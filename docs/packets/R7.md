## Deliver

`Coordination/*`: a small enum of reaction modes (animated, balancing, bracing, falling, recovering), a pure arbitration function (inputs: requests with priorities and hysteresis state; output: the one owning mode and why), per-mode blend weights over joints, and the locomotion stance and gain intent. ## Check

- Exactly one mode owns the character at any tick; lower priority requests never override a higher one.
- A mode change fades gains over a stated time instead of switching instantly; the sum of per-joint ownership weights is 1.
- A target change moves the drive target but never produces a position teleport (no output contains a position).
- Deterministic under repeated identical input.
