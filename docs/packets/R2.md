## Entry point

```text
static BalanceReport BalanceController.Tick(in BalanceParams parameters, ref BalanceControllerState state, ReadOnlySpan<RoleBody> bodies, ReadOnlySpan<SupportProbe> feet, in BalanceInput input, Span<JointDrive> drives)
```

`feet` has two entries (left, right). `drives` has `Roles.Count` entries indexed by role. `BalanceInput`: timestep, `WideStep`, an optional desired height. `BalanceReport`: `Supported`, `SteppingLeg` (or none), `StepInProgress`, `StepDistance`, `Decision` (`Standing`, `Stepping`, `Falling`), `ComHeight`. The decision `Falling` means the controller gave up (abort condition), not that the body is on the ground.

## Check, using the harness (`tests/SA.Harness`, `docs/harness.md`; add a project reference)

1. Stand, no push, 10 s. Target: pelvis within 0.12 m of its start height, torso within 15 degrees of upright.

3. Push 400 N·s. Target: the report says `Falling` and stays so.
4. Two identical runs give identical drives (bit for bit).
5. Hostile input (NaN, infinity, short spans) gives passive drives and a finite report.

If a target is not met, do not weaken it or the harness. Report the measured values, the likely limiting factor (harness torque, gains, a port adaptation) and what you tried.
