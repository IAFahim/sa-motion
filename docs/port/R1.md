# Port record R1: balance core

Upstream: Delirium Physics, revision `8e4ad4e`,
file `crates/delirium/src/nmdbm.rs`, lines 1-843 only.
Lines 843 onward (body adapter and tick) are packet R2, not translated here.

## Method notes, stated once

- Ordinary float math everywhere. No `fma` ordering, no `frsqrte`/`vrefp` reciprocal
  estimates, no Newton refinement, no axis-order byte strings (`sdot` orderings such as
  `b"yzx"` are plain dot products). `atan2f` (threshold approximation) is replaced by
  the full `atan2`.
- `Vec3` becomes `SA.Motion.Numerics.Float3`, `Quat` becomes
  `SA.Motion.Numerics.Quaternion`, `M34` (three axis columns plus position) becomes
  `SA.Motion.Mechanics.Frame` (rotation plus position). Engine types (`Character`,
  `Ragdoll`, `Collision`, `Ctx`, `Body`, `Build`) never appear. Inputs are
  `ReadOnlySpan<RoleBody>` plus one `SupportProbe` per foot; outputs are
  `Span<JointDrive>` plus small result state the caller owns.
- The source humanoid has 21 parts: pelvis, four spine segments, neck, head, two
  clavicles, and upper arm, forearm, hand, thigh, shin, foot on each side. The 15-role
  target collapses the three upper spine segments, the neck, and both clavicles onto
  `Torso`. The spine effector reads the `Torso` body; the pelvis part reads the
  `Pelvis` body; limbs map directly.
- Every `kv!(n)` tuning slot becomes a named `BalanceParams` field with an authored
  starting value and a one-line reason below. No game data is read; the runtime runs
  with no GTA data present.
- Upstream joint-angle helpers `nmrs::angles` and `nmrs::rotation` sit outside the
  cited range, so the lean/twist convention here is standard mechanics, marked ADAPTED:
  lean angles tilt the joint-local +Z axis, twist spins about local +Z. The two stay
  consistent because `HipLeans` and `JointRotation` are written against each other.

## Excluded and adapted

- `rage_link`, `link`, `logged_links`, `DERIVED_LINKS`, `HOMER_PARTS` 66-93: excluded.
  Engine link tables plus an environment-variable file override next to the unlock path.
- `step_clamp` mode scaling 95-97: excluded here, R2 owns the mode switch.
- `horizontal` 185, `atan2f` 238: unused inside lines 1-843 except by R2 code; omitted.
- Flag words: the source packs touch codes and step state into `Snap.flags` and
  `Feet.flags` with data-bank masks. Masks are unresolvable without game data, so R1
  uses explicit booleans (`LeftContact`, `RightContact`, `LeftPlanted`,
  `RightPlanted`, `SteppingLeg`, `InsideSupport`, `NearFootStep`, `NoContactStep`).
  `decide` keeps the countdown expiry shape; the opaque free bit is dropped.
- `limb_ik` 636 and `limb_ik_w` 642 wrappers: ADAPTED and folded into `Solve`. They
  move effector records between engine structs; R1 takes the limb frame and the gains
  directly from the snapshot and the params.
 - `m34` 826, `own_rage` 830, `Body` 843 onward: R2. `SnapshotBuilder.TryBuild` is the
   `snap` equivalent, ADAPTED: COM and velocities are inverse-mass-weighted means of
   the role bodies, and the root frame is the COM with the pelvis rotation composed
   with a fixed +90° X turn, reproducing the no-rigid-data fallback frame in `block`
   (lateral, forward, down), because the rigid-solver block average is engine
   internals.
 - Joint frames are procedural: each hip/knee frame keeps its body position with
   rotation built by the shortest arc taking local +Z down its distal segment, with
   segment lengths from body spacing. Actual angles are measured in that convention
   (hip axial twist, knee flexion from the segment angle). ADAPTED: the rigid-solver
   joint frames are unavailable.
 - Drive conversion is correction-form: joint frame times desired relative rotation
   times inverse actual relative rotation through the joint-to-body offset. Limit
   centres apply to normalisation only. The RAGE bind frame this replaces is engine
   data.
 - `predict_knee` uses the project-local lean/twist convention instead of
   `nmrs::rotation`. ADAPTED, consistent with `SolveLeg` by construction.
 - Ankle swing pitch uses the authored `AnkleSwingOffset` instead of
   `acos(leg_length)`. ADAPTED: a leg-length cosine is solver tuning, not geometry.
 - Ankle yaw carries the leg side factor, matching the hip path. The cited lines
   804/808 omit it; without it ankle drives break the mirror check.
 - `decide` steps the foot with the larger step distance: for a lateral shove that is
   the foot on the side the shove comes from. Touchdown is the stepping foot grounded
   while the stance foot unloads, or the separating-and-close case.
 - The stance re-basing in `balance_solve` is ADAPTED to world-frame stance: the
   `cm`/`cd` form needs the solver pelvis-frame convention, which is outside the
   cited range, and it displaces planted targets at zero tilt. The replacement keeps
   the `reach` clamp in the pelvis frame with world targets. `pv.lift` stays R2.
 - `lead` floor uses the named `MinLeadDistance` (0.07 m).
 - No opaque fields remain: `p148`, `p152`, `p168`, `p172` are named from verified use;
   `abort`, `taper`, `low_abort`, `contact`, `leg_stiffness` are carried by name for R2.

## R2b amendments (owned folder, R2 change requests)

- `BuildLeg` frames are joint-based (hip/knee/ankle estimated from segment
  midpoints); lengths are hip-to-knee and knee-to-ankle, matching source
  `leg_ik`. Foot `Frame` stays at the body center; the ankle frame sits at
  the ankle joint. Absolute `SplitRotation` lean actuals were tried and
  reverted: procedural frames follow segments, so the absolute rotation
  carries the constant body-mount offset; only twist is observable without
  a bind reference, and `Hip.Actual` stays twist-only.
- Forward-axis rule (see R2b record in `docs/port/R2.md`): body-derived
  frames use local +Y as the horizontal length/forward axis.
- `Decide` carries the source free gate; `SupportInsideMargin` is 0.0.
- New `BalanceParams.AnkleClearance` (Standard 0.0: stateless fixtures stay
  sole-direct; the R2 tick seeds bind ankle height). `PelvisHeight`
  Standard is 1.0 (measured harness bind).
