Arithmetic note: the source relies on console float behaviour in a few helpers.
This port uses ordinary float math throughout and does not reproduce it.

## Basis and units

World basis, metres, seconds, radians. Directions are unitless unit vectors.
`Float3`/`Quaternion` are `SA.Motion.Numerics` types. The source `M34` part frames
collapse to body position plus rotation. Engine types (`Character`, `Ctx`,
`Collision`, `Obstacle`, `HeadLook`, `SpineTwist`, effectors, joints) never appear.
Inputs are `ReadOnlySpan<RoleBody>` indexed by `(int)Role`, an explicit world `up`
vector, a fixed timestep, and caller-owned forward probes. Outputs are
`Span<JointDrive>` indexed the same way plus a small intent struct.

Body-frame conventions authored for this port: torso forward is local +Z, torso up
is local +Y, an arm hangs along local -Y, a hand palm faces local +Y. These match
no upstream table; they are stated here so the aim math has a fixed meaning.

## Role collapse (21 parts onto 15 roles)

Four spine segments and `SPINE3()` collapse onto `Torso`. `HEAD()` and neck
effectors collapse onto the `HeadTarget` point; no neck drives are emitted.
Clavicle and shoulder-clavicle joints do not exist as roles; `clav_follow`
becomes an extra roll on the upper-arm `Target` scaled by `ClavFollowRatio`.
Arm chains map shoulder to `UpperArm`, elbow to `Forearm`, wrist/hand to `Hand`,
per side. Hip, knee, ankle, and spine stiffness writes are excluded: this slice
emits drives for the six arm roles only and leaves every other slot `Passive`.
Leg pose memory (`leg`, `lean2`) is kept in state for a future slice and does not
drive output yet.

## Excluded and ADAPTED

- Caller casts (`cast`, `ray_box`): excluded. The caller supplies one forward
  `SupportProbe` plus one touching flag per arm in `CatchFallProbes`.
- Native limb IK (`limb_ik`, yaw/lead/Vel inputs): ADAPTED. Each arm role gets an
  aim rotation built from ordinary vector math between its current limb direction
  and the clamped target point. Same reach clamp, same contact-averaged target.
- Wrist response (`Shot::wrist`): ADAPTED. On a probe hit the hand drive aims at
  the contact with full arm authority; on a miss it holds a low-stiffness curled
  pose (`SafeMissStiffness`), matching the source safe-miss branch shape.
- Elbow clamp on backward falls: ADAPTED. When falling backward the forearm
  target stays straight with the upper arm; otherwise it carries an `ElbowRest`
  bend about the aim-normal axis.
- Spine and neck effector writes (curl, lean, twist decay, neck track):
  excluded from drives. The slice reports `HeadTarget` (`head + dir`) and a
  capped `SpineTwist` angle about world up instead.
- Head-look and spine-twist controllers: external. `UseHeadLook` gates whether
  `HeadTarget`/`SpineTwist` carry intent or stay neutral.
- `touched` latch and neck tracking after contact: kept as the `Touched` latch;
  neck effector writes become the head intent above.
- Kinetic-energy gain: same shape, unit mass. `ke` is approximated from torso
  speed (`sqrt(2 ke)` equals torso speed at unit mass).
- `st.twist` clavicle branch: excluded with the clavicles; the fold uses the live
  aim error instead of stored random leans.
- Unresolved: none. Every kept field is named after verified use. Opaque game
  tuning values were never read; all standards are authored above.
