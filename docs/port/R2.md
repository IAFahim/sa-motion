## Method notes, stated once

- Ordinary float math everywhere. No `fma` ordering, no `vrefp`/`frsqrte`
  estimates, no Newton refinement, no axis-order byte strings.
- `Vec3` becomes `Float3`, `Quat` becomes `Quaternion`, `M34` becomes
  `Frame`. Engine types (`Character`, `Ragdoll`, `Collision`, `Ctx`, `Body`,
  `Build`) never appear. Inputs are `ReadOnlySpan<RoleBody>` (length
  `Roles.Count`) plus two `SupportProbe` entries (left, right). Outputs are
  `Span<JointDrive>` (same indexing) plus `BalanceReport`.
- The 21-to-15 collapse is R1's (three upper spine segments, neck, clavicles
  onto `Torso`); the tick adds no new body mapping.
- `BalanceParams` tuning slots keep R1 names and `Standard` values. New
  named quantities introduced by the tick live in `BalanceTickTypes.cs` or as
  named constants on `BalanceController`, each with a one-line reason below.
  No game data is read; the runtime runs with no GTA data present.

## Countdown and flags

- `Countdown` expiry: R1 `Decide` decrements to `-1` and suppresses new
  steps at `<= 0`. The tick treats `Countdown == -1` as give-up in `Holding`,
  matching source `holding`, and latches `Falling`.
- `WasAlone`, `WasFar` (set at step start), `NearFootStep`, `NoContactStep`
  (set while stepping) are R1 `BalanceFeet` fields; the tick persists them by
  keeping `BalanceFeet` inside `BalanceControllerState` across ticks.
- `Lift`, `BlendVelocity`, `Centre` are R1 `BalancePelvisState` fields kept
  in the same state. `Centre` is assigned the pre-placement feet centre before
  `PelvisControl.Update` (source order). `BlendVelocity` tracks snapshot
  horizontal velocity unless `FixedVelocity` is set, mirroring source `pv.vel`.
  `Lift` is assigned `HeightTarget` after `Solve` (source `phase3 pv.p68`).
- `abort`, `taper`, `low_abort`, per-foot `contact`, `leg_stiffness` stay
  `BalanceParams` fields (source `Params`): `abort`/`low_abort` feed `Holding`,
  per-foot contact feeds `TryBuild`/`Update` via the probes, `LegStiffness`
  scales the per-tick copy servo strengths, `taper` feeds the knee post-pass.
- `FallingLatched` is new tick state: once `Holding` gives up, every later
  tick returns `Falling` with passive drives.

## `step_clamp` mode switch (nmdbm.rs 95-97, nm.rs 431)

`ch.mode == 2` exactly when the figure is the MARGE build, else `1`. Mode 2
scales the step clamp by `kv!(70)`. There is no MARGE build here, so the
caller selects the wide-stance variant with `BalanceInput.WideStep`
(`false` = mode 1, `true` = mode 2). The multiplier is the authored constant
`WideStepFactor = 1.5`: the wide-stance figure is allowed fifty percent more
reach before the step target clamps. Slot 70 is not read from game data.

## Drive ownership

The tick writes `JointDrive` only for `Pelvis`, `Torso`, `ThighLeft/Right`,
`ShinLeft/Right`, `FootLeft/Right`. Legs and spine come from R1 `Solve`; the
pelvis entry is new (below). Arms, hands and head stay `Passive`.

The pelvis entry is ADAPTED: the source has no pelvis orientation drive (the
rigid-solver root is positioned by the engine). The harness pelvis entry is a
world torque with no position servo, and an undriven pelvis whiplashes under
torso corrections, so the tick emits an upright-anchor drive: target keeps
the current heading while removing tilt, `TargetAngularVelocity` zero (damp
relative spin). Gains are the authored constants `PelvisStrength = 2000` and
`PelvisDamping = 60`: the strength dominates the heaviest child drive so the
light pelvis anchors the torso, the damping is above the critical value on
the pelvis-torso coupled inertia. Torques stay bounded by `MaxTorque`.

## Taper post-pass (source `apply` knee branch)

ADAPTED: the source modulates solver-internal knee effector strength per leg
from knee flexion when `taper` is set. R1 `Solve` takes one shared
`KneeStrength`, so the tick scales the emitted knee `Stiffness` by the square
of a flexion factor from the snapshot knee actuals with authored constants
(`TaperBendReference`, `TaperRate`, `TaperMinimum`). Off at `Standard`
(`Taper = false`).

| taper curve | `TaperBendReference`, `TaperRate`, `TaperMinimum` (consts) | 0.2 rad, 2.0, 0.5 | flexion past reference bends strength down to half, straight knees keep full strength |

## Excluded and adapted, all in one place

- `m34`, `own_rage`, `Body::rigid/part/pmat/eff3/eff1/limb/at/zero_place/foot/block`:
  engine frame plumbing; inputs arrive as `RoleBody` spans, joint frames are
  R1 procedural frames. Excluded.
- `Dbm::{new, restart_over, tilt, lean_vector, lean_force}`: lean-message and
  contact-edge bookkeeping with no caller input; excluded. Up stays `+Y`.
- `Dbm::{acc, leg_offset, knee_scale, ratio, event, success, last, down}`:
  solver telemetry and bind-pose offsets; excluded. `held` maps to
  `Report.Supported`; `lean_vec`/`as_force`/`push` are zero.
- `probe` world raycast and `gvel` filter: the caller probes; excluded beyond
  what R1 `BuildSupport` carries.
- `activate` effector gains and `oppose_gravity`: engine internals; our drives
  carry stiffness/damping and the harness clamps torque. Excluded.
- `apply` knee `muscle_stiffness`/`oppose_gravity` terms: same reason; only the
  taper strength shape survives as the post-pass. `ch.support` flags: no
  `Character`; excluded.
- `phase3` ankle clamp: subsumed (see tick order); `Lift` carry kept.
- Ankle over-travel redistribution stays R1's.

## R2b amendments (repair, all measured on the H1 harness)

- `JointDrive.Target` is the desired world orientation of the driven body
  (see `docs/contract.md`); `WriteLegDrives` knee term used `+e` and flexed
  backwards, fixed to actual-minus-desired (A/B: fall at 55 vs 175 ticks).
- Foot convention is sole-plane: `Update` targets stay in sole space, `SolveLeg`
  converts to ankle space with the seeded `AnkleClearance` (bind ankle height,
  0.10 m here); the +0.05 probe lift is removed as redundant.
- Leg frames are joint-based (hip/knee/ankle from segment midpoints, shin is
  knee-to-ankle as in source `leg_ik`); the old center-based shin (0.265 m)
  could never span hip height.
- `BalanceParams.PelvisHeight` 0.95 to 1.0: measured bind straight-stand pelvis
  height on the harness; 0.95 commands a 39 deg knee crouch from a 1.00 start.
- `Decide` free gate restored (`free = !InsideSupport`, source `decide`); the
  `SupportInsideMargin` -0.18 to 0.0 makes the gate functional. The R2 tick
  0.09 m step deadband stays as second-layer hysteresis defense.
- Planted ankles anchor to a world-flat reference (`FlatAnchor`: shortest arc
  taking foot local +Z to down, same pattern as the pelvis/torso anchors);
  swing ankles keep the follow composition. ADAPTED: the source anchors via
  solver effectors.
- Body-frame forward axes: source foot/root `C` columns are the horizontal
  length axis, which is local +Y on our body-derived frames (foot length,
  root forward per the `block` fallback lateral/forward/down). `Support`,
  step-target, sole, toe and swing-forward computations use +Y; procedural
  Arc frames keep length on +Z. `Support.Measure` horizontalizes both axes
  so identity test frames and pitched harness frames share one ground quad.
