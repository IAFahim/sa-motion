## Priority table (arbitration ladder, highest first)

| Mode | Requests when | Priority |
|---|---|---|
| Falling | `fallen` latch set, balance lost | 40 |
| Bracing | brace, catch-fall, high-fall, or shot/hit active | 30 |
| Recovering | roll-up, get-up, or pedal/writhe active without a fall | 20 |
| Balancing | balance controller active | 10 |
| Animated | locomotion or animation tracking owns the joints | 0 |

Priorities are data on each request, so callers may separate ties; arbitration keeps the current owner while a challenger leads by no more than the hysteresis margin.

## Part collapse (21 source parts onto 15 roles)

Spine segments above the pelvis collapse onto `Torso`; the pelvis segment is `Pelvis`; clavicle segments collapse onto the matching `UpperArm`; neck and head segments collapse onto `Head`.
