Arithmetic uses ordinary float math throughout. No console-arithmetic emulation (`fma` ordering, `frsqrte`, `vrefp`, bit-exact estimates are not reproduced).

Up is world `+Y`. Facing is the torso-rotation `+Z` axis. Both are authored conventions; the source reads them from engine state we do not receive.

## Part-to-role collapse

21 source parts onto 15 roles: `PELVIS` onto `Pelvis`; `SPINE2`/`SPINE3` and the two remaining spine segments onto `Torso`; each arm chain shoulder onto `UpperArm`, elbow onto `Forearm`, wrist/hand onto `Hand`; clavicle effector detail folded into the `UpperArm` drive; legs, feet, neck and head untouched (legs/feet: R1; head/neck: head-look packet).

## BalanceHint

Replaces the `Dbm` coupling (`fixed_vel`, `vel`, `leg_offset`, `acc`). R1 produces the real values later.

| Field | Unit, basis | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| `Support` | 0..1, 1 = fully supported | scales guard stiffness; clamped, non-finite reads as 0 |
| `Lean` | signed, torso pitch sense | biases `LeanIntent`; clamped, non-finite reads as 0 |

## Unresolved

- Exact engine touch-bitmask semantics (kv91/kv7): which contacts count as bracing touch is the caller physics engine decision, carried by probe `Hit` flags.
- Absolute stiffness scale: gains are authored for the `JointDrive`/`QuaternionDrive.Torque` consumer and have not been tuned against a solver.
- Head-look target blending while braced: owned by the head-look packet.
