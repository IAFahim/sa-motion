revision `8e4ad4e`. Covers `Params`, `Out`, `BodyBalance` (`activate`, `tick` at

## 21-part to 15-role collapse

Source joint groups resolve through the engine joint table (`nmrs.rs`, indices
from tuning slots, structural only): 4 spine segments, 2 neck, 2 clavicle,
2 shoulder, 2 elbow, 2 wrist, 2 hip, 2 ankle, plus singles. Collapse used here:

| Source group | Roles written or read |
|---|---|
| 4 spine segments (bias triple, twist coupling, stiffness) | `Torso`: pitch bias averaged to `TorsoPitchBias`, four twist couplings replaced by single `TwistCorrect`, stiffness folded into torso drive |
| 2 neck (oppose gravity, zeroed desired) | `Head`: stabilise-hold drive, look intent carried in output only |
| 2 clavicle (blend to zero, oppose gravity) | `UpperArmLeft/Right`: proximal arm drives |
| 2 shoulder (hip-coupled desired) | `UpperArmLeft/Right` |
| 2 elbow (hip-coupled + arms-out + bend) | `ForearmLeft/Right` |
| 2 wrist (zeroed) | `HandLeft/Right`: passive hold |
| 2 hip (desired/actual read only) | `ThighLeft/Right` read as orientation only; twist proxy is pelvis-vs-torso yaw |
| 2 ankle (touch bits) | `FootLeft/Right` not read; `BalanceInput.Grounded` replaces the touch bitmask |

Lower-body roles keep whatever drives the caller placed; only the seven upper-body
roles above are written. Pelvis and Torso orientations are read for the own-frame,
lean, twist and turn computations.

## Adapted engine dependencies (standard-mechanics replacements)

- `Character.set_stiffness` strength/damping convention (`s*s`, `2*s*d`) maps to
  `JointDrive.Stiffness`/`Damping` with the same formulas, capped by `MaxDrive`.
- `Effector.desired` euler triples (spine/neck/shoulder/elbow/wrist) map to
  corrective local rotations composed onto the current role orientation; absolute
  joint angles have no meaning without a bind pose.
- `muscle_stiffness`, `oppose_gravity`, `blend_to_zero` fold into drive
  stiffness scaling by the speed-derived authority factor.
- `doll.kinetic` speed maps to pelvis linear speed; `snap.vel` maps to pelvis
  velocity; `snap.com` maps to the pelvis position.
- `doll.collided("ub")` maps to `BalanceInput.ContactUpper`; foot touch bitmask
  maps to `BalanceInput.Grounded`; hip desired/actual maps to the pelvis-vs-torso
  yaw proxy; `ch.busy` maps to the grounded/stepping gate; `ch.random` maps to a
  fixed-seed deterministic generator owned by the state.
- `Obstacle::find` maps to nearest-hit probe within `LookProbeRange`.
- `stabilise` maps to the `HasStabilise`/`StabiliseStiffness`/`StabiliseDamping`
  output triple for the caller's solver.
- World up is +Y. The own-frame/euler derivation is the Y-up analogue of the
  source Z-up derivation.
